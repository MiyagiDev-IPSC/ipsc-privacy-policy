Privacy Policy

IPSC Competency Test

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

IPSC Competency Test is an Android application developed by Miyagi Dev, designed to record, calculate, manage, and store results from IPSC competency tests.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information Processed by the App

The application allows users to enter and manage information, including:

  • Participant names and test details.
  • Shooting scores, hit results, and test results.
  • Test dates and related information.

All information is entered voluntarily by the user.

3. Data Storage

All information entered into the application is stored locally on the user's device.

The application uses local device storage to save and manage test results.

No personal information is automatically transmitted to the developer's servers.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

IPSC Competency Test does not collect personal data for the developer.

The application does not automatically share user information with third parties.

No user account or registration is required.

5. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The application's core functionality works without an internet connection.

The application does not use advertising networks, external analytics services, or tracking technologies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Test results remain stored locally until deleted by the user or removed through the device's application data management.

Users can delete saved test results directly within the application.

Users can also remove locally stored information by clearing the application's storage data or uninstalling the application, subject to device functionality.

7. Data Security

Information is stored locally on the user's device.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices against unauthorized access.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for use in connection with organized shooting sports and competency testing.

It is not specifically designed for children.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data handling practices change.

Any changes will be reflected in the updated policy.
